_or, Things Better Left Unsaid_

On a cotton field, on a sunny day, I hear the sound of gravel slipping beneath the tires as strangers drive past this untouched, unheard piece of land I bought last year.

"What is this for?" you ask.

"You have to be quiet."

Whoa.

A family of squirrels runs past me.

For the past few years, I have been living my midlife crisis in my twenties. The thing about the present is that no one really lives in it. We all crave something old, something new, something strange. We all do this.

We humans have a strange connection to the past, wanting to fantasize about it, reliving it again and again, even though it never truly was how we imagine it to be—how we remember it as something so memorable.

"You scared them away."

_Shhh._

The human heart is so strange. It craves validation where we don't actually need it. It craves others as though we ourselves are incomplete on our own. We do incomprehensible things just to be the center of someone else's attention. It is not just love.

Throughout human history, throughout our whole lives, we have craved judgment. I think it is already engraved in our frontal lobe. We have been redesigned this way.

"I really like those cotton fields."

It is so strange. You wouldn't think of beauty and imagine a cotton field. But here I am.

Or maybe it's just the lack of colors that draws your eyes sometimes, when everything revolving around you is so clustered, so vivid. Well, technically, they are colorful—just not yet.

If you think that way, see how we try, once again, to dream of the future through even the most mundane things.

I wonder which color dye they will get. Which person is going to wear them, make them part of his, her, or their personality, and eventually stain them with memories, textures, and time.

They are colorful—just not yet.

"Things take time, bro."

"We don't have the time."

That's how things eventually end up for everyone.

Unless you say you are done when you aren't finished.

There's so much more to come—the good, the bad, the ugly, the beautiful, the vivid dreams, the times you will dwell on the past and the future, thinking, reminiscing about how you used to feel.

But are those thoughts just flash memories you crave in the present?

_Tossy, tossy._

**The art of leaving things halfway.**
